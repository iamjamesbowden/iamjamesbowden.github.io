This site sat as a placeholder for longer than I would like to admit. It now has an up-to-date list of my publications, the projects I am working on, my teaching, and the knowledge exchange and commercialisation side of my job, which has grown a lot over the last couple of years.

<!--more-->

I plan to use this blog for shorter pieces that do not fit neatly into a journal article or a white paper. That will include notes on new papers and what they found, methods I use in financial text and audio analysis, things I learn from teaching fintech to a very international cohort, and the occasional comment on news in financial services and regulation.

If any of it overlaps with your own work, or you are thinking about a PhD in this area, please get in touch.
