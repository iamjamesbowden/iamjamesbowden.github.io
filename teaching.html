I have taught at Strathclyde Business School since 2019 and am Programme Director of the MSc Financial Technology. I designed the two postgraduate modules I lead, and I try to keep both close to what is happening in industry and research. I was shortlisted for Best in Faculty (Business) at the Strathclyde Teaching Excellence Awards in 2021 and 2022.

Modules

  • AG945

    Fintech Innovations, Applications and Considerations

    MSc Financial Technology, semester 2

    Each week takes one area where technology is changing financial services, such as artificial intelligence, natural language processing, open banking, crypto-assets and RegTech, and looks at how it works, who is using it and what can go wrong. Students help choose the topics before the schedule is fixed.

  • AG952 / AG983

    Textual Analytics for Accounting and Finance

    MSc programmes in accounting and finance, September and January intakes

    How to turn text into data. Students work with dictionary methods such as Loughran and McDonald, topic models, document similarity and transformer models, and apply them to real corporate disclosures, including the risk factor sections of 10-K filings.

  • AG443

    Data and Textual Analytics for Accounting and Finance

    Honours year, both semesters, co-taught with Dr Panagiotis Loukopoulos

    Builds the technical and analytical skills needed to work with both structured financial data and unstructured text in accounting and finance.

Previously: Corporate Financing (honours, until 2025); Finance and Financial Management (Glasgow and Dubai); Future of Fintech (MSc Strategic Fintech, Bahrain).
