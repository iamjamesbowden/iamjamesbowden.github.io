I am Departmental Director of Knowledge Exchange in Accounting and Finance and lead the Commercialisation Task Force at Strathclyde Business School. My own work with industry falls into three parts: commercialising our research, partnerships with firms and public bodies, and engagement with policy.

Commercialisation

I am involved in three projects going through the University of Strathclyde's stage-gated commercialisation process. They are in multimodal AI, explainable AI and trade finance. More details will follow.

Awards

  • 2026Stephen Young Entrepreneurship Award for an Outstanding Business Idea Research Paper, University of Strathclyde. £25,000 towards the commercial development of a multimodal AI project.
  • 2025RegTech Innovation award, Scottish FinTech Awards, won by the Financial Regulation Innovation Lab.

Industry partnerships

  • Financial Regulation Innovation Lab

    Co-investigator since 2023 on FRIL, led by FinTech Scotland with Strathclyde and the University of Glasgow and funded by Innovate UK and, from 2026, Scottish Enterprise. FRIL runs innovation calls for fintechs and publishes applied research with industry partners.

  • Nature Broking Ltd

    Knowledge Transfer Partnership funded by Innovate UK (2026–2028), with Dr Kyung Yoon Kwon and Professor Dimitris Andriosopoulos. It brings financial modelling, risk and scenario analysis and AI into the developing market for natural capital.

  • Callala Ltd

    UK Space Agency-funded work to develop PEEPS, a satellite-based environmental performance rating for offshore assets.

  • The Weir Group

    Partner on a European Space Agency-funded PhD studentship on mapping future environmental degradation with earth observation and machine learning.

  • IAAER and KPMG

    Research grant on the disclosure of unrecognised goodwill, with findings presented to IASB members and KPMG.

  • University College Dublin

    Scottish Government-funded feasibility study on a Scotland–Ireland partnership in satellite data (2024–2025).

Policy and public engagement

I took part in the Department for Business and Trade's Regulation Roadshow roundtable in 2025, and hosted a visit by the House of Commons Treasury Select Committee to Strathclyde the same year. I organise events that bring together industry, academics and public bodies, including a 2023 event on climate finance, space data and resilient timing with the National Physical Laboratory, and FRIL's workshop on generative AI at the Scotland FinTech Festival.

My commentary on markets and fintech has appeared in The Conversation UK, the World Economic Forum, MarketWatch, Business Insider and on France 24. Selected pieces are listed on the publications page.
